\chapter{Finding companies}\label{ch:discovery}

\section{Where the search starts}
Tab \ui{2. Location and start} takes an address (looked up with Nominatim),
coordinates, the home town found in the CV, or a point from the start-point dialog
(paste from a map link, or latitude/longitude moved by an azimuth and a distance).
Then an \textbf{area mode}; what is required depends on it:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Radius around the point}: the radius must be a number above 0 (slider 0.5--50\,km, any positive value
        can be typed). A typed address that is not found is refused. With an \emph{empty} address the search starts
        from the chief town of the selected province or, without one, of the selected region, and says so next to
        the button.
  \item \textbf{Whole province} / \textbf{whole region}: the address is optional. Choose the \ui{Region} (default
        Emilia-Romagna) and, for a province, the \ui{Province}; with no address the search starts from the chief town
        or the regional capital. \ui{Whole province} without a province is not started: the app asks you to choose one.
\end{itemize}


A typed address that cannot be found is \emph{ignored} in the two wide modes (with a note) and the centre is
used. \ui{Show on map} shows the start point and
radius (section~\ref{sec:map}). Without a profile with a role \ui{Start} does not run (section~\ref{sec:autosave}).

\section{Sources}
Independent sources run behind one interface (\code{discovery/sources.py}), local ones first, at most two
network sources at a time; a failure is non-fatal and each reports a status line. All requests share one polite gateway: 2\,s between requests to a host, two hosts at once, cached answers, one back-off on HTTP 429 and then the host is dropped. Tab~2 ticks local map data, the
regional register, Overpass and Wikidata by default.

\textbf{Imported lists} (CSV/XLSX: Registro Imprese lists, the innovative start-up register,
any list with a name) are no longer a source of their own: \ui{Import company list\ldots} stores
them \emph{in the register}, where they can be edited and refreshed (section~\ref{sec:lists}).
Twenty Italian region extracts are listed. Download your region once: searches
then answer in seconds, also offline. Optional job boards (Indeed, LinkedIn via JobSpy) are \textbf{off}.

\section{Merge, categories, ranking}
\textbf{Merge.} Duplicates are joined by VAT or tax code, then website domain, then name with town or a position
within 300\,m; provenance is kept per field.

\textbf{ATECO 2025 categories.} The app proposes up to 15 categories \emph{of interest} from the profile
(ISTAT table, CC BY 4.0); you edit them. A company's own ATECO code is never guessed: it comes only from the register.

\textbf{Ranking before any site is read.} A score from free local data: sector terms and profile words in the name,
industrial OpenStreetMap tags, Wikidata industry, register ATECO inside or outside the chosen categories, a small
bonus for a website, demotion of public offices, schools, shops and associations.
A register ATECO \emph{inside} the chosen categories lifts the score to at least 0.95 and
adds 0.3; a known code \emph{outside} them costs 0.25; an unknown code costs nothing.
A company whose \emph{stored} revenue figures grow gets a \textbf{small bonus}: $+0.05$ from
$+2$\,\%/year, $+0.10$ from $+10$\,\%/year, never negative and never hiding a company
(rule in section~\ref{sec:trend}); only for companies with a card holding three yearly values. Reasons are stored as \textbf{``why selected''}; \emph{Maximum companies} keeps the top.
